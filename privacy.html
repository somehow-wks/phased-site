Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

Phased has no account, no sign-up and no servers of its own. Your goals, notes and history live in the app on your device, and we never see them.

Where your data lives

  • On your device Your plan is stored on your device and works fully offline.
  • In your iCloud If you are signed in to iCloud, Apple keeps a copy in your private iCloud storage so your own devices stay in sync. Nobody else can read it - including us.
  • In your exports A file is made only when you tap Export, and goes only where you choose to save it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled on your device from the plan you wrote. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Nothing is collected

Phased has no analytics, no ads and no trackers. It does not collect, share or sell any personal data.

Payments

Any purchase in Phased is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete a goal and it disappears from every device on your iCloud account. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device; the iCloud copy stays until you remove Phased's data in iCloud settings, and would come back if you reinstall.

Questions

Anything unclear, or anything you want deleted? Write to phased.support@gmail.com.
