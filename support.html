Where is my data?

Your plan lives in the app on your device and works fully offline. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs automatically between your own devices through your private iCloud - there is no sync button, iOS syncs whenever it can. Settings shows the current sync status.

How do I keep a copy of my plan?

Settings, then Your plan, then Export. That saves one small file holding your whole plan - keep it in Files or anywhere you like. Import on the same screen brings it back, on any device, with or without iCloud.

How do I move my plan to a new iPhone?

Sign the new phone in to the same iCloud account and your plan arrives on its own. No iCloud? Export a file on the old phone, send it over, and import it on the new one.

A notification did not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Phased in iOS Settings, and that the goal itself is allowed to notify under Settings, Notifications, Goals in the app.
